# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and teams at small and medium-sized businesses in Spain, across sectors, who want to reduce repetitive work, improve handoffs between tools, or understand where AI and automation fit. They may not know which technical approach they need and should be able to explore a process without entering personal, customer, or sensitive data.

## Product Purpose

VARINO explains, designs, builds, and documents practical automation and AI systems around real business processes. The website helps a visitor understand the offer, explore a clearly labelled demonstration, describe a process at a high level, and choose a human-reviewed next step. Success means a visitor can judge fit, scope, price range, data handling, and human approval points without being led to believe an unverified client result or deployment exists.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, approachable. VARINO should feel capable and forward-looking without jargon, pressure, or claims it cannot evidence. The intended experience is clear and composed, with the quality of Apple interfaces as inspiration for hierarchy and interaction, while preserving VARINO's own identity.

## Anti-references

- No terminal-as-decoration, unfinished-looking demos, dead controls, or motion that hijacks scrolling.
- No invented clients, reviews, savings, performance metrics, deployments, integrations, guarantees, or legal assurances.
- No generic SaaS card grids, decorative gradients, or AI-generated sales language that obscures what a service actually includes.
- No collection or transmission of personal or sensitive customer data through an illustrative guide or demo.

## Design Principles

1. Show the process: explain what enters, what the system prepares, where a person decides, and what outcome is actually delivered.
2. Make proof legible: distinguish demonstrations from client work and describe capabilities only when they are available and verified.
3. Give the visitor agency: let them compare services, explore a process, switch themes, and contact a person without forced motion or a single funnel.
4. Make commercial boundaries clear: show indicative ranges, VAT, exclusions, third-party costs, and human approval points before commitment.
5. Earn trust through restraint: preserve VARINO's calm visual identity, protect readable contrast and reduced-motion preferences, and avoid unverified claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text contrast, keyboard operation, focus visibility, semantic structure, and touch targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences, support light and dark themes, avoid scroll hijacking, and keep core content available if client-side scripts or the local AI service are unavailable. Accessibility conformance remains subject to testing; this document is a target, not a compliance claim.
